About Apostle Silas

A voice for truth. A heart for people.

For more than two decades, Apostle Silas M. Okoro has devoted his life to proclaiming the Gospel and strengthening leaders across Africa, Europe, and North America. His call to ministry began in 1999 while serving in local church outreach, and today he leads The Covenant Assembly alongside a growing family of partner churches.

Educated in theology and pastoral leadership, Silas combines deep scriptural study with accessible teaching. His ministry has included church planting, leadership schools, humanitarian service, and international conferences attended by people of many generations and cultures.

At home, he is a husband, father, mentor, and lifelong student of Scripture. His philosophy is simple: sound doctrine should produce compassionate people, healthy churches, and restored communities.
